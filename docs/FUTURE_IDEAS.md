# Future ideas (saved, not built yet)

## Merch and print on demand, per channel

The goal is to turn the channels into brands that sell gear, mainly for the kids channel (Wonder Hollow Workshop), and to see which channel and which item earns money.

What the owner wants:

- A Shopify print on demand store, using the look and characters of each channel: t-shirts, mugs, posters and other gear.
- Stuffed animals based on the characters, if a vendor can be found.
- Sales info inside the app, per channel and per item.
- Trackable links for merch, so a link placed in a video description can be traced to the sales it brings. Wonder Hollow cannot use this on YouTube, because the channel is made for kids, but the other channels can.
- One place to manage it all, so the app feels omni channel.

Rough shape when we get to it:

1. Brand kit per channel (logo, colors, character art) already lives in the brand assets. Reuse it for product mockups.
2. Connect Shopify (Admin API) to read products, orders and sales. Connect a print on demand partner (Printful or Printify) through Shopify.
3. A Merch tab: products by channel, sales by channel, by item and by week.
4. Link builder: short links with a channel and video tag, added to descriptions on channels that allow links. Sales are matched back to the video through the tag.
5. Kids channel rule: no shopping links in anything on a made for kids channel.

Open questions for later: which print partner, the shop domain, and whether to sell stuffed animals through a separate vendor.
