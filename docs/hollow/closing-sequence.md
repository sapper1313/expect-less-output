# Wonder Hollow Workshop: Closing Sequence (short version for the Writer)

The closing is a finished, premade video (closing_15s.mp4) placed at the end of every episode. It is never generated per episode and never written into a script. It has no credits, no text and no buttons, because YouTube turns off cards and end screens on made for kids videos.

- Length: 15 seconds, the same song as the opening, slower and softer, like a lullaby at dusk in the forest clearing.
- What it shows: all four friends gather at dusk and sing a goodbye, wave, the camera rises toward the stars, then the Wonder Hollow Workshop logo fades to dark.
- The song (original, do not quote other songs): "Thanks for coming to the Hollow, wave goodbye and off you go! Sharing, caring, and counting too, see you next time, we'll count with you! See you in the Hollow!"
- The sign-off line "See you in the Hollow!" is sung by the whole crew in the closing. Nobody says it in the episode itself.

What this means when writing an episode:
- The last scenes of the episode are only Juniper's spoken teaser for the next episode, with the friends in the workshop. Do not write a goodbye, a sign-off line or a song in the episode.
- The closing begins right after the teaser, outdoors at dusk, so end the episode on a calm, warm note.
- Never ask viewers to click, tap or subscribe. Put links in the video description for parents.
- For compilations, the closing plays once at the very end only.
