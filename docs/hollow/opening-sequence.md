# Wonder Hollow Workshop: Opening Sequence (short version for the Writer)

The opening is a finished, premade video (opening_20s.mp4) placed at the start of every episode. It is never generated per episode and never written into a script. The episode script starts right after it, so do not write an opening scene and do not repeat its words.

- Length: 20 seconds, a bright morning welcome song at 96 BPM.
- What it shows: the empty forest clearing at golden hour, Old Maple's glowing lantern, Juniper waving, Widget counting one, two, three, the three friends hopping on their names, a group cheer, then the Wonder Hollow Workshop logo.
- The song (original, do not quote other songs): "Lanterns glow in the hollow tree, so come on in, count one, two, three! Juniper, Widget, Biscuit too, math and kindness, see what we do! Wonder Hollow Workshop!"

What this means when writing an episode:
- Start with the story's first scene (the cold open: the small problem appears). Do not add a title scene, a welcome, or a song.
- The theme says the show is about counting, math and kindness. Keep every episode true to that.
- For compilations, the opening plays once at the very start only.
